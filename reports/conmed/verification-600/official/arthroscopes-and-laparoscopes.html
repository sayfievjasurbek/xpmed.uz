                                CONMED's versatile portfolio of arthroscopes and laparoscopes are autoclavable, designed to promote efficiency and durability in multi-specialty procedures.
                            

                        

                    

                

            

        

    

    

        

            

                


                    









ARTHROSCOPES












    
    
  • QuickLatch® Ultra-High Definition (UHD): 4mm autoclavable arthroscopes and accessories provide increased clarity and detail for vivid image quality. UHD scopes have a 157mm working length and sapphire lens.
    • 
    
  • High Definition (HD): 4mm scopes feature state-of-the-art optics with an innovative scope-sheath interface. HD scopes have 170mm WL.
    • 
    
  • QuickLatch® 2.9mm arthroscopes offer equivalent light transmission and image size to 4mm scope
    • 
    
  • Proprietary sheath system designed to increase durability
    • 
    
  • 152mm WL, 100° field-of-view, and 0°, 30°, 45°, 70° angle-of-view
    • 



















LAPAROSCOPES












    
    
  • Third-generation Ultra-High Definition (UHD) scopes
    • 
    
  • Designed to enhance image clarity and sharpness for improved anatomical viewing
    • 
    
  • 5.5 and 10mm sizes are available in standard or extended lengths, with 0°, 30°, 45° angle-of-view
    • 



















STERILIZATION TRAYS












    
    
  • Allows for proper storage and reprocessing of CONMED’s HD autoclavable devices
    • 
    
  • Can accommodate camera heads, scopes, sheaths, bridge systems, cannulas, and light guides
    •
