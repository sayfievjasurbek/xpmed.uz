## Verification
All 80 pages cached at 600 DPI. Product pages use Tesseract PSM3/11 and Apple Vision. Isolated PSM7/13 and Codex visual readings resolve disagreements. Original readings, coordinates, rejected aliases, and source page images are retained. No T-prefix substitutions were used.
Official confirmation checks exact codes in https://www.conmed.com/-/media/conmed/documents/catalog/hallpoweredinstruments_catalog.ashx; the current manufacturer catalog is a different edition, so its extra products were not imported.
Raw catalog code candidates, repeated picture inscriptions and compatibility references are not reported as separate ordering items.

## Images

Only caption-matched visible photos are used; hidden PDF objects are not exposed. White blade drawings are 600-DPI source crops, never rembg. Gray drawing shading and the source bold-code chromium legend determine coating.
